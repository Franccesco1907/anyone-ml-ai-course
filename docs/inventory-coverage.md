## What an inspection establishes

Course and module folders follow the actual observed order. Each syllabus records stable lesson IDs, titles, platform completion markers, content kinds, resource references, and coverage. Public metadata was compared with the private initial sidebar snapshots; unknown markers remain unknown.

A page-container inspection identifies its displayed resource links and embedded viewers. It does not mean the linked notebook was executed, the PDF body was read, or the video was watched. Some notebook and project links lead to Drive or Colab; file formats behind opaque links remain unverified. A DOCX download link was directly observed in job preparation.

Eight quiz entries and the Technical Challenge Orders Quiz survey entry have sidebar-only coverage. Other assessment shells encountered during browsing were not answered or submitted. No completion control was clicked; final course percentages matched the initial dashboard.
