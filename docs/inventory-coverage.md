## What an inspection establishes

Course and module folders follow the actual observed order. Each syllabus records stable lesson IDs, titles, platform completion markers, content kinds, resource references, and coverage. Public metadata was compared with the private initial sidebar snapshots; unknown markers remain unknown.

A page-container inspection identifies its displayed resource links and embedded viewers. It does not mean the linked notebook was executed, the PDF body was read, or the video was watched. Original files reached through Drive, Colab, and direct download links were acquired and format-checked where available. File-by-file statuses appear in the [material catalog](../resources/materials.csv). A PDF supplied as slides remains a PDF; no native PowerPoint file is implied.

Eight quiz entries and the Technical Challenge Orders Quiz survey entry received read-only attachment checks. Assessments were not answered or submitted. One Data Science video automatically completed during player inspection; its original incomplete marker was restored. A fresh course view then matched all 40 original lesson markers (26 completed). Reading or downloading resources is separate from demonstrated understanding.
